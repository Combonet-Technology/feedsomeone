Transparency and Governance

Open, responsible and accountable

Building public trust through clear information

Oluwafemi Ebenezer Foundation is a Nigerian nonprofit registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission. We share clear information about our identity, programmes and community work so supporters, communities and partners can understand who we are and how we operate.

Legal registration

  • Legal name: Oluwafemi Ebenezer Foundation
  • CAC registration number: 179189
  • Date of incorporation: 27 April 2022
  • Country of registration: Nigeria

What we publish

  • Clear descriptions of our programmes and activities.
  • Impact updates grounded in completed activities and available records.
  • Clear distinctions between completed work and future programme direction.
  • Corrections when previously published information is incomplete or inaccurate.

Governance and accountability

OEF operates as an Incorporated Trustee under Nigerian law. Questions about governance, partnerships or publicly available foundation information may be sent to info@oluwafemiebenezerfoundation.org.

Transparency FAQ

Is Oluwafemi Ebenezer Foundation registered?

Yes. OEF is registered in Nigeria with the Corporate Affairs Commission. CAC registration number: 179189. Incorporation date: 27 April 2022.

What is Feed Someone?

Feed Someone is OEF's flagship outreach programme. It began as a community relief movement in 2019 and now sits under the registered foundation.

Where does OEF operate?

OEF is registered in Nigeria and has documented outreach history in Akure, Ondo State, with additional founder-led and partner-supported work in other Nigerian communities.

What programmes does OEF run?

OEF focuses on practical relief, dignity-focused care, community support, health awareness, safe lives, learning support, social support and future digital opportunity programmes.

How can people contact OEF?

Supporters, communities and partners can contact info@oluwafemiebenezerfoundation.org for partnership, volunteer, programme or media enquiries.

Where can people learn about OEF's work?

OEF shares completed outreach records and community stories through the Impact and News pages.
